## Maya
Stylised low-poly 3D character with soft faceted surfaces, visible polygon facets and matte clay-like shading.

- **Head and face:** a large round head and warm tan skin. Big round black glasses, small dark dot eyes, short dark brows, a gentle smile and small gold hoop earrings.
- **Hair:** shoulder-length, voluminous, wavy dark-brown hair in chunky low-poly locks. It has a side part and a fringe swept across the forehead, with a small teal rectangular hair clip on one side.
- **Outfit:** an open dark-green jacket over a mustard-yellow top, charcoal trousers with rolled cuffs and white sneakers.
- **Hands and proportions:** simple rounded hands and cute chibi-like proportions.

## Claude
A small low-poly 3D mascot with coarse visible facets and a matte finish.

- **Colour:** bright orange.
- **Shape:** a rounded flower or cloud body with about seven soft lobes all around its silhouette.
- **Face:** two tall black rectangle eyes, with no mouth and no arms.
- **Feet:** two small round feet.
- **Size:** about the size of a coffee mug.

## Scene (daily life)
- **Setting:** a cosy night desk with a large window behind. The window shows a dark-blue night city with warm lit windows and a bookshelf.
- **Desk:** a wooden desk with a monitor showing charts, a laptop or keyboard, a white mug on a yellow coaster, a notebook and pen, and a potted plant.
- **Light:** a warm desk lamp, with the warm lamp light against the cool night, and a shallow depth of field.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, in the same low-poly faceted 3D style.
